Why does Credit Risk arise?

Credit Risk

Deals with Ability to Pay Issue on part of the borrower
Change in financial condition of the borrower over time
Incorrect Ability to Pay assessment by the lender at the time of Underwriting

Fraud Risk

Usually forms part of Operational Risk

Willingness to Pay Issue on part of the borrower
03

Individual vs Institutional Credit Risk

Individual Credit Risk
  • Lending to a person
  • E.g. of Credit Risk Indicator: CIBIL score of the person
Institutional Credit Risk
  • Lending to a company or institution
  • E.g. of Credit Risk Indicator: ICRA or CRISIL Rating of a company
04

Significance of Credit Risk

Profitability of Lender

  • P & L impact
    • Write Offs
    • Provisions

Regulatory Requirements

  • Meet Capital Requirements
  • Need to estimate Credit Losses for accurate Capital related calculations

Pricing of products to optimize Risk Return Trade Off

  • Risk based pricing strategy i.e., higher risk segments should be priced higher to hedge against the incremental risk

Ability to raise Capital

  • Well managed Credit Risk helps in raising capital (more investors, better rates, ratings)

Overview on AML (Mostly part of Compliance Risk)

  • Anti-Money Laundering (AML) refers to a set of policies, regulations, and procedures that financial institutions and other regulated entities implement to prevent, detect, and report activities that aim to disguise illegally obtained funds as legitimate income
  • Objective is to combat aspects like Terrorist Financing and different types of illegal activities. KYC is an important aspect of AML Strategy
StageDescriptionExample
PlacementIllicit funds are introduced into the financial system.A drug dealer deposits small amounts of cash into multiple bank accounts to avoid detection.
LayeringThe money is moved through complex transactions to obscure its origin.Transferring money through shell companies or using cryptocurrencies to hide the source.
Integration"Cleaned" money is reintroduced into the legitimate economy as lawful income.Purchasing luxury real estate or investing in a business to legitimize the illicit funds.

Examples of Credit Risk Questions at different stages

Acquisition

  • Are some acquisition channels inherently bringing in high risk customers? (e.g. Direct Selling Agents)

Underwriting

  • Are rules and scores approving disproportionately higher volume of risky customers?
  • Is the loan assignment framework allocating higher loan compared to the credit worthiness of the customer?

Customer Management

  • Is engagement/exposure increasing for the low risk customers?
  • Is exposure being controlled for high risk customers?

Collections

  • What differentiated collection strategy to apply across different customer groups to maximise payments/recovery?
